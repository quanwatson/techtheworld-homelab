# 16-career/

Career-track artifacts produced by the training program — evidence of progress that isn't tied to a single certification.

- `skill-matrix-history/` — dated snapshots of the skill matrix shown on the dashboard, so growth over time is visible and not just the current-state view.
- `monthly-reviews/` — a short monthly check-in against the plan: what shipped, what slipped, what the next month's focus is.
- `proof-of-work-index.md` — running index of proof-of-work records (see `17-ots/proof-of-work/`) worth citing in interviews or performance reviews.
